321 College Football

3… 2… 1… GO! The 1v1 college football knowledge battle.

How to play

Before GO, both players lock in a school. School vs School: name a player who was on both rosters. School vs Pro: one player picks a school, the other a pro team (roles swap every round), and you name a player who went from that school to that team. Choose Power conferences + Notre Dame or all FBS schools on the home screen. First to 3 rounds wins. Ranked battles swap trophies (3–0: ±11, 3–1: ±10, 3–2: ±8), and the weekly leaderboard resets every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

FAQ

I can't start a ranked battle. Sign in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app (Settings › Game Center), then reopen 321 College Football. Practice mode works without signing in.

I lost trophies when I left a battle. Leaving, closing the app or going offline mid-battle counts as a 0–3 defeat (−11).
