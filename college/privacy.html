321 College Football — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

321 College Football ("the app") is a free college football trivia game made by Sarp Dündar. This policy explains what data is involved when you use it.

Data we collect

We do not run our own servers, accounts or analytics, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal data ourselves.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen and an occasional full-screen ad between battles. Google may collect and process data from your device to deliver, measure and improve ads, including the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data.

  • On first launch you may be asked for ad consent (required in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states). You can change your choice anytime in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you decline, you still get ads, just not personalized ones.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Game Center

Online battles, friend invites and leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Apple shares your Game Center display name with players you are matched with, and your trophy scores are submitted to Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

Your trophy count, win/loss record and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them; your trophy total can be restored from your Game Center leaderboard score.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
