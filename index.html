321 Football

3… 2… 1… GO! The 1v1 football knowledge battle.

How to play

Before GO, both players lock in a club. Once both picks are revealed, the first to name a player who played for both clubs wins the round. First to 3 rounds wins. Ranked battles swap trophies (3–0: ±11, 3–1: ±10, 3–2: ±8), and the weekly leaderboard resets every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

FAQ

I can't start a ranked battle. Sign in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app (Settings › Game Center), then reopen 321 Football. Practice mode works without signing in.

I lost trophies when I left a battle. Leaving, closing the app or going offline mid-battle counts as a 0–3 defeat (−11).

A correct answer wasn't accepted. Email us the two clubs and the player, and we'll fix the database in the next update.

How do I change my ad choices? In the app: Settings › Privacy choices.
